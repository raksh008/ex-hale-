Ex-hale

Breathe out. You don't have to get over it alone.

Ex-hale is a gentle community for people going through a breakup, or stuck on someone they can't let go of.

What it's for

Breakups are lonely, and friends get tired of hearing about it. Ex-hale is a space where you can say what you're feeling and be heard by people who know exactly how it feels.

What we're planning

Post anonymouslyShare how you feel without your name attached. Pick an avatar and be yourself, without the pressure.
Talk with people who get itJoin small, moderated chat rooms for people at similar stages of healing.
Small steps, not a timelineSimple check-ins and reminders to take care of yourself, at your own pace.
No judgment. No pressure to move on. Just a place to breathe.
